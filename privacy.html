2. Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At stgames.online, accessible from https://stgames.online, protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document details the types of data collected and how we use it.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to https://stgames.online and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Cookies & Local Storage

ST Games uses standard browser localStorage and cookies to store non-sensitive user preferences, such as your bookmarked favourite games, sound settings, dark/light theme preference, and high-score progress. Optional player sign-in and cloud leaderboards are powered securely by Google Firebase Authentication.

Log Files & Google Analytics

stgames.online follows a standard procedure of using log files and Google Analytics. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts to analyze trends and improve game performance. None of this data is linked to personally identifiable information.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
