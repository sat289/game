Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ST GAMES ("we", "our" or "us") handles information when you visit https://stgames.online/, including its browser games and related pages.

Site-owner reminder: Keep this page accurate whenever analytics, sign-in, databases, advertising or other integrations are added or removed. Do not describe a feature as active unless it is actually enabled.

1. Information processed

Depending on the features used, information may include basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP-derived approximate location, pages visited, referring page and request time. Hosting and service providers may process this information to deliver and protect the site.

Game preferences, favourites, scores and progress may be stored in browser local storage. This information can remain on your device until you clear the browser's site data. If account, sign-in, online leaderboard or database features are enabled and used, the relevant provider may process account identifiers and information submitted for those features.

If you contact the site through a linked social account, we receive the information you choose to send.

2. Cookies and local storage

Local storage may remember game progress, favourites and interface settings. Cookies or similar technologies may be used by third-party services for advertising, measurement, security and service delivery. You can manage or clear cookies and site storage through your browser settings; doing so may affect some features.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

ST GAMES may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure and personalise ads, subject to your location, consent choices, browser settings and Google's policies. These technologies may process information such as IP address, browser identifiers, ad interactions and information about visits to websites or apps.

Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps, Google's advertising privacy information and Google ad settings.

4. Analytics

If Google Analytics or another analytics tool is enabled, that provider may collect page views, interactions, device and browser details, approximate location and usage events. This information may be used to understand and improve the website. See Google's Privacy Policy. The presence of this section does not mean analytics is active if it has been disabled.

5. Third-party services and links

The website may link to or use services operated by other organisations, including advertising, analytics, fonts, content delivery networks and social platforms. Those providers process information under their own policies. We do not control external websites or their privacy practices.

6. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the website, remember game settings and progress, provide account or leaderboard features when enabled, respond to messages, understand performance, display and measure advertising, prevent abuse and protect the service.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker business. Information may be processed by service providers when needed to provide their services, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the website and its users.

8. Retention and security

Browser-stored information generally remains until you clear it. Information held by account, database, advertising or analytics providers is retained according to their settings and practices. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

ST GAMES does not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information to play games. Any account, advertising or other feature used by children must follow applicable age, consent and child-directed service requirements. Parents or guardians may contact us using the official contact channel below with a privacy concern.

10. Your choices

  • Use browser settings to clear cookies and local storage.
  • Use Google's ad settings and privacy controls to manage available advertising choices.
  • Review and revoke third-party account permissions through the relevant provider, where available.
  • Contact us with a privacy question or request.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when site features, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ST GAMES site operator through the official ST GAMES Instagram account. Do not send passwords, verification codes or other sensitive information.
