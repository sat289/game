Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ST GAMES ("we", "our" or "us") handles information when you visit and use https://stgames.online/, including its browser games and related pages.

Please note: The services used by a website can change. This policy describes the types of information that may be processed by features currently used or enabled on ST GAMES. The site owner should keep this page accurate whenever analytics, sign-in, databases, advertising or other integrations are added or removed.

1. Information processed when you use the site

Depending on which features you use, information may include:

  • Basic technical information: browser type, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, pages visited, referring page, and date or time of a request. Web servers and service providers may process this information to deliver and protect the site.
  • Game and preference data: game progress, high scores, settings, favourites and similar information saved in your browser's local storage. This data may remain on your device until you clear the browser's site data.
  • Account information: if Google sign-in or another account feature is enabled and you choose to use it, the identity provider may share basic account details such as a user identifier, display name or profile image. The exact information depends on the permissions shown during sign-in.
  • Database information: if online profiles, scores or leaderboards are enabled through Firebase or another backend, the information submitted for those features may be processed and stored by that service.
  • Messages: if you contact us through a linked social account or another contact method, we may receive the information you choose to send.

2. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

ST GAMES and its service providers may use browser storage technologies. Local storage can remember game progress, preferences and interface settings. Cookies or similar technologies may be used by third-party services for functions such as advertising, measurement, security and service delivery. You can manage or clear cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Disabling them may affect some site features.

3. Google AdSense and advertising

ST GAMES may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure and personalise ads, subject to your location, consent choices, browser settings and Google's policies. These technologies may process information such as IP address, browser identifiers, ad interactions and information about visits to this and other websites or apps.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps. You can review Google's advertising privacy information and ad settings. Ad personalisation and available controls vary by region and account status.

4. Analytics and measurement

If Google Analytics or another analytics tool is enabled on the site, that provider may collect information about page views, interactions, device and browser details, approximate location and usage events. This information helps understand site usage and improve the service. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices. If analytics is not enabled, this section does not mean that analytics data is being collected.

5. Third-party services and links

The site may link to or use services operated by other organisations, including Google sign-in, Firebase, Google advertising, analytics, fonts, content delivery networks and social platforms. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites. Please review their policies before using their services.

6. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the website, remember game settings and progress, provide account or leaderboard features when enabled, respond to messages, understand site performance, display and measure advertising, prevent abuse, and protect the security of the service.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker business. Information may be processed by the service providers described above when needed to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the site, its users or others.

8. Data retention and security

Browser-stored information generally remains until you clear it. Information held by account, database, advertising or analytics providers is retained according to the settings and retention practices of those services. We use reasonable measures appropriate to a small website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

ST GAMES does not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information to play games. If an account, advertising or other feature is used by a child, applicable age, consent and child-directed service requirements must be followed. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information in a way that should be reviewed can contact us using the social contact link below. We will review the request and take appropriate steps where required.

10. Your choices

  • Use your browser settings to clear cookies and local storage.
  • Use Google's ad settings and privacy controls to manage available advertising choices.
  • Review and revoke third-party account permissions through the relevant provider, where available.
  • Contact us to ask a privacy-related question or request a review of information associated with a site feature.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when site features, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published. Please check this page periodically.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ST GAMES site operator through the official ST GAMES Instagram account. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or other sensitive information in a message.
