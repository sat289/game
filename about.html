About Us

Last updated: 15 November 2026

ST GAMES is an independent online gaming website at stgames.online. Our tagline is Skill | Strategy | Victory.

The website brings together browser-based games across arcade, classic, racing, sports and two-player battle categories. Visitors can browse the game catalogue, search for titles, choose categories and launch available games directly in a web browser.

What you can find here

  • Free-to-play browser game experiences.
  • Game categories and search to help visitors discover titles.
  • Favourites and locally stored game preferences on supported browsers.
  • Game guides and instructions where provided with a title.

Our purpose

Our aim is to make casual browser gaming easy to discover and accessible across phones, tablets and computers. Individual games may have their own controls, features, creators and technical requirements.

Who operates ST GAMES?

ST GAMES is maintained by its site operator, Satish Kommana. The website is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers or publishers of third-party games unless expressly stated.
