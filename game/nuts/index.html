🔩NUTS AND BOLTS

Move nuts between the bolts until all
the nuts on each bolt are the same
colour!

You can only move nuts onto other
nuts of the same colour.

HOW TO PLAY

Tap a bolt to lift its top nut. Tap another bolt to move it. You can only place a nut on an empty bolt or on the same colour.
