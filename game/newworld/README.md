# Follower Nagaram 3D (v6 — 5–7 Floor Modern Towers, Zero-Overlap Guard, Free-Roam Pan/Zoom & ⚡ Pro Reel Tour)

Upload the `follower-town/` folder into your `sat289/game/` GitHub repository.

## What's New in v6
1. **Zero-Overlap Hard Guard:** Overlapping houses inside Police HQ, Cricket Stadium, or any 4/8-plot landmark are now blocked at the render level AND can be permanently relocated in Firebase via **🌤️ World -> 🛠️ Fix & Relocate All Overlapping Houses**.
2. **5–7 Floor Realistic Follower Towers:** Replaced small boxes with multi-story modern towers modeled after your 3 architectural references (6-floor brick & green balcony tower, 6-floor staggered cantilevered cube tower, 5-floor luxury stone/wood-louver LED tower, and 7-floor golden glass tower).
3. **Free-Roam Google-Maps Pan & Zoom:** 1-finger drag pans smoothly anywhere across the city; 2-finger pinch zooms & rotates right where you are instead of snapping back to one spot.
4. **⚡ Pro Reel Fast-Motion Animation:** Inside **🎬 9:16 Reel**, tap **⚡ Pro Reel** for an automatic high-speed cinematic zoom-in/out tour highlighting ST Junction, your major landmarks, and your newest added follower towers with animated banners.
